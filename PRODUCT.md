# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user choice). No build step; deployable to any static host.

## Users

People in Buenos Aires who discover El Rey Del Panini through Instagram (@elreydelpanini) or by walking through La Mala / Paseo de La Infanta, mostly on their phones, deciding where to eat on a Friday or Saturday night.

## Product Purpose

Website for El Rey Del Panini, a new Italian panini stand. It has to show the brand exactly as the physical place looks, let visitors read the full menu, and tell them when and where to go.

## Positioning

"Autentici panini italiani": Italian panini (mortadela con pistachos, stracciatella, porchetta) plus New York and Detroit style pizza, with a gluten-free (sin TACC) panini option and focaccia bread on request.

## Operating Context

- Open Fridays and Saturdays, 20:00 to 00:00.
- Located inside La Mala: Av. del Libertador 3883, Arco 4, Paseo de La Infanta (Palermo, CABA).
- Partners shown on the menu: Aperol, Fiambres Calchaquí, Fiambres Bocatti.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Menu content comes verbatim from the Instagram "Menú" highlight (assets/ig/menu0-2.jpg).
- No prices are published except the Aperol promo ($10.000 to add an Aperol Spritz to a panini or pizza). Do not invent prices.
- No online ordering, reservations or delivery links are known. Do not invent them.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: the king doing the "chef's kiss" gesture, with an animated version (assets/src/el-rey-del-panini-beso-v3.mp4). The animated logo must be the first thing visitors see and it should move.
- Colors from the menu and storefront: cream #EEE9CB, bottle green #465E46, checkered red #992637, brick red #8B4141 (logo wordmark).
- Type: soft heavy Cooper-style serif for headings (menu pills, wordmark); condensed bold italic sans for item names; plain sans for descriptions.
- Red and cream gingham borders, green awning with cream lettering, wood paneling.
- Footer must include "Hecho por Santi Viñas".

## Evidence on Hand

- Photos and video frames from the brand's own Instagram posts and reels (assets/ig/, assets/img/).
- Instagram bio: "Autentici panini italiani". Inauguration post: "Casa llena, corazón contento."
- No testimonials, press, ratings or founding story are available. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Look like the place: a visitor who has been there recognizes it instantly.
2. The menu is the product; it must be complete and readable on a phone.
3. When and where must be findable in one glance.
4. Only real facts; leave out anything the brand has not published.
